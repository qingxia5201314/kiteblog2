![2026-09-19-23-40-25-Snipaste_2026-09-19_23-39-08.png](/media/migrated/img-91a7ee11-dc7d-45fa-a114-c9f556af4197.png)

发现是输入的每个字符去异或0x78，那么一样的密文异或0x78会还原，编写exp

```python
enc b'OJN\x1b\x1a\x1c\x1aHULLI\x1dUL\x1c\x1dKU\x1a\x19KOULO\x1cHKJ\x1e\x1do\x1a@\x19
enc bytes(i 0x78 for i in enc)
print(enc)
```
